# Thaikootam Travel - Featured Tour Packages & Experiences

## 1. Keralam Backwaters & Misty Hills (6 Days / 5 Nights)
- **Tagline:** Experience "God's Own Country" — from the cloud-kissed tea estates of Munnar to tranquil emerald backwaters.
- **Price:** Starting from ₹24,999 INR ($299 USD) per person (Twin sharing).
- **Destinations Covered:** Kochi (Fort Kochi heritage), Munnar, Thekkady (Periyar Wildlife), Alleppey (Alappuzha Backwaters).
- **Handpicked Highlights:**
  - Traditional private luxury houseboat stay in Alleppey with freshly prepared Kerala Sadya and Karimeen Pollichathu.
  - Guided walking tour through lush tea gardens in Munnar with tea tasting session.
  - Evening Kathakali dance and Kalaripayattu martial arts cultural performance in Thekkady.
  - Sunset stroll by the Chinese Fishing Nets and Jew Town in Fort Kochi.
- **Inclusions:** 4-star boutique hotel and eco-resort stays, 1 night in a deluxe AC private houseboat, daily Kerala breakfast and dinner, private chauffeured AC vehicle for all transfers, spice plantation entry fees.
- **Exclusions:** Flights / train tickets to Kochi (COK), personal Ayurvedic wellness treatments, optional bamboo rafting at Periyar.

## 2. Hyderabad Royal Heritage & Culinary Trail (4 Days / 3 Nights)
- **Tagline:** Step into the opulent era of the Nizams, fragrant saffron biryanis, and centuries of architecture.
- **Price:** Starting from ₹14,500 INR ($175 USD) per person (Twin sharing).
- **Destinations Covered:** Charminar, Golconda Fort, Chowmahalla Palace, Qutb Shahi Tombs, Salar Jung Museum, Hussain Sagar Lake.
- **Handpicked Highlights:**
  - Curated Old City Food Trail: Authentic Hyderabadi Dum Biryani, double ka meetha, and Irani Chai with hot Osmania biscuits at iconic heritage cafes.
  - Evening Sound & Light spectacle at the historic Golconda Fort.
  - Private heritage tour through Chowmahalla Palace, the official seat of the Nizams.
  - Optional day excursion to Ramoji Film City (the world's largest film studio complex).
- **Inclusions:** 4-star heritage accommodation in Banjara Hills / Begumpet, daily breakfast, private AC transfers with local storytelling guide, all monument entry tickets.
- **Exclusions:** Airfare to Hyderabad (HYD), shopping at Laad Bazaar for bangles and pearls, personal dining expenses outside the guided food walk.

## 3. Amsterdam Canals & Dutch Countryside (5 Days / 4 Nights)
- **Tagline:** Cruise 17th-century golden-age canals, cycle past historic windmills, and dive into vibrant Dutch art.
- **Price:** Starting from €1,150 EUR ($1,250 USD) per person (Twin sharing).
- **Destinations Covered:** Amsterdam City Center, Jordaan Canal District, Museumplein, Zaanse Schans Windmill Village, Volendam & Marken.
- **Handpicked Highlights:**
  - Classic sunset canal cruise with artisan Dutch cheese and warm Stroopwafels.
  - Skip-the-line entrance tickets to the Van Gogh Museum and the Rijksmuseum.
  - Half-day Dutch countryside excursion to Zaanse Schans to see working historic windmills, artisanal cheese dairies, and wooden clog workshops.
  - Vintage bicycle rental with a friendly local guide through the hidden courtyards (Hofjes) of the Jordaan district.
- **Inclusions:** 4-star boutique hotel along the Amsterdam canal belt, daily European buffet breakfast, public transport GVB 72-hour city travel card, museum tickets, and windmill tour transport.
- **Exclusions:** International flights to Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS), Schengen tourist visa fees, optional Keukenhof Tulip Gardens trip (available March to May).

## 4. Bali Tropical Escape & Island Bliss (5 Days / 4 Nights)
- **Tagline:** Turquoise beaches, volcanic landscapes, and spiritual tranquility in the Island of the Gods.
- **Price:** Starting from $799 USD per person (Twin sharing).
- **Destinations Covered:** Ubud (Culture & Arts), Seminyak (Beachfront), Nusa Penida Island, Tanah Lot Temple.
- **Handpicked Highlights:**
  - Private day trip via speedboat to Nusa Penida: visit Kelingking T-Rex Beach, Angel's Billabong, and Crystal Bay.
  - Sunrise tour through Tegallalang Rice Terraces and the famous Ubud Jungle Swing.
  - Spectacular sunset seafood feast on the sand at Jimbaran Bay.
- **Inclusions:** 4-star boutique villa accommodation with private pool option, daily breakfast, private AC airport transfers, speedboat tickets, English-speaking Balinese driver-guide, all temple permits.
- **Exclusions:** International flights to Denpasar (DPS), Indonesian Visa on Arrival ($35 USD), lunch and dinner unless specified.

## 5. Japan Golden Route & Cultural Odyssey (8 Days / 7 Nights)
- **Tagline:** Bullet trains, serene bamboo groves, neon skyscrapers, and ancient shrines.
- **Price:** Starting from $1,890 USD per person (Twin sharing).
- **Destinations Covered:** Tokyo, Mount Fuji / Hakone, Kyoto, Nara, Osaka.
- **Handpicked Highlights:**
  - Authentic Ryokan overnight stay with private thermal onsen bath and multi-course Kaiseki dinner in Hakone.
  - Guided morning walk through Kyoto's 10,000 vermilion torii gates at Fushimi Inari Shrine and the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove.
  - Scenic bullet train (Shinkansen) journey with views of Mount Fuji.
  - Street food safari in Osaka's lively Dotonbori district (Takoyaki and Okonomiyaki tasting).
- **Inclusions:** 7-day Japan Rail (JR) Pass, 4-star hotel stays in Tokyo and Kyoto, traditional Ryokan night in Hakone, breakfast daily, pocket Wi-Fi device for unlimited connectivity.
- **Exclusions:** International flights to Tokyo (NRT/HND), Japanese tourist visa, travel insurance, personal shopping in Akihabara or Ginza.
