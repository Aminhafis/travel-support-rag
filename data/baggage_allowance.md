# Thaikkudam Travel Co. - Baggage Allowance & Restrictions

## Cabin Baggage (Carry-On)
- **Economy Class:** 1 carry-on bag with maximum weight of 7 kg (15 lbs), plus 1 small personal item (laptop bag, handbag, or small backpack). Dimensions must not exceed 56 cm x 36 cm x 23 cm.
- **Business & First Class:** 2 carry-on pieces totaling up to 14 kg combined (neither piece exceeding 8 kg).
- **Liquid Restrictions:** All liquids, aerosols, and gels in hand baggage must be in containers of 100 ml (3.4 oz) or less, packed in a transparent, resealable 1-liter plastic bag.

## Checked Baggage
- **Domestic Flights (Standard Economy):** 1 checked bag up to 15 kg included.
- **International Flights (Standard Economy):** 2 checked bags, each weighing up to 23 kg (50 lbs). Maximum total linear dimensions: 158 cm (length + width + height).
- **Excess Baggage Charges:** Extra weight at airport check-in is charged at $15 USD per extra kg on domestic flights, and $25 USD per extra kg on international flights.
