# Thaikootam Travel - Baggage Allowance & Restrictions

## Cabin Baggage (Carry-On)
- **Economy Class (Domestic & International):** 1 carry-on piece with maximum weight of 7 kg (15 lbs), plus 1 small personal item (laptop bag, handbag, or small backpack). Dimensions must not exceed 56 cm x 36 cm x 23 cm.
- **Business & First Class:** 2 carry-on pieces totaling up to 14 kg combined (neither piece exceeding 8 kg).
- **Liquid Restrictions:** All liquids, gels, and aerosols in cabin baggage must be in containers of 100 ml or less, enclosed within a clear resealable 1-liter plastic pouch.

## Checked Baggage
- **Domestic Flights within India (Kerala / Hyderabad routes on IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet):** 1 checked bag up to 15 kg (33 lbs) included per passenger.
- **International Flights (Amsterdam, Europe, Japan, Bali):** 2 checked bags, each weighing up to 23 kg (50 lbs) on standard economy tickets. Maximum total linear dimensions: 158 cm (length + width + height).
- **Excess Baggage Fees:**
  - Domestic routes: ₹550 INR (~$7 USD) per extra kg if booked at the airport, or ₹400 INR per kg if pre-purchased via Thaikootam Concierge 6 hours before departure.
  - International routes: $25 USD per extra kg. Special 25% discount available when added during package booking.
